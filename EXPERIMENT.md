# 35-day evaluation

- Launch date: 2026-08-02 JST
- Decision date: 2026-09-06 JST
- Acquisition boundary: Tool Shelf, GitHub, search discovery, and natural sharing only
- Excluded from demand: automated QA and operator smoke tests

Read the funnel in order:

1. unique non-QA visitors;
2. visitors selecting a major category;
3. visitors searching by business term, name, or code;
4. successful and zero-result searches;
5. hierarchy-filter changes;
6. classifications saved or copied;
7. official detail pages opened;
8. returns after at least eight hours.

If visits do not lead to category selection or search, improve the entry map and examples. If zero-result searches are frequent, improve normalization and search guidance before adding data. If results are viewed but not copied or opened officially, improve the hierarchy and action order. If no real users arrive, review natural discovery and pause feature expansion.
